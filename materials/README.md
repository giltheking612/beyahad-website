# Source materials — official ביחד (Beyahad) party site

Pulled directly from https://be-yahad.org.il/ on 2026-10-19, for staff/volunteer
use building this site. Not for redistribution outside that purpose.

- `logo/logo-official.png` — official logo, extracted from page schema.org data.
  Brand colors sampled from it: primary blue `#00aaff`, navy `#0a0084`,
  accent orange-red `#ff4b00`.
- `team/team-list-all-35.json` — all 35 candidates: name, age, city, role tags,
  photo URL, profile URL. Scraped directly from the team archive page HTML
  (not AI-summarized).
- `team/team-bios-full-10.json` — full structured bios (age, city, family
  status, expertise, "about me", personal note) for candidates 1–10, scraped
  from each person's individual profile page.
- `team/photos/` — official headshots for candidates 1–20, numbered by their
  listed order (01 = Bennett, 02 = Lapid, …).
- `policies/*.txt` — cleaned verbatim text (tags stripped, not AI-summarized)
  from the 6 flagship policy pages used on this site: giyus (draft reform),
  yokermichya (cost of living), education, housing, transport, health.

Election: Knesset elections October 27, 2026. Party "ביחד" (Together),
led by Naftali Bennett and Yair Lapid, founded 2026-04-26 as a merger of
Bennett's party and Yesh Atid. Campaign tagline: "רק ביחד נתקן את ישראל"
("Only together will we fix Israel").
